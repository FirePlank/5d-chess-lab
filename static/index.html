Rules

The rules Kairos plays by: 5D Chess With Multiverse Time Travel, with the community tournament standard for stalemate (exiled kings).

The game Turns Timelines Check and mate Exiled kings Endings

The game

  • Boards are up to 8×8. Pieces: king, queen, rook, bishop, knight, pawn, and the fairy pieces princess, unicorn, dragon, brawn, royal queen and common king.
  • Castling and en passant work as in the original game. Pawns and brawns promote on the last rank, to a queen in the standard variants.
  • Kings and royal queens are royal. A common king is not.
  • Variants: every variant of the official game (Standard and its versions, Simple, Small, Very Small, Misc, Focused and Checkmate Practice) except Random and Global Warming.

Turns

  • The present is the earliest latest-board among the active timelines. The side whose board it is moves.
  • An action is one move on every playable board on the present, plus any moves on boards further on where it is also that side's turn. Then the side submits.
  • An action is legal only if the mover's royal pieces cannot be captured afterwards.

Timelines

  • Moving onto a board that is no longer the latest of its timeline creates a new timeline from that board.
  • White's new timelines are numbered up from the main one, Black's down.
  • A side may create at most one more active timeline than its opponent. Any timeline beyond that is inactive: it stays on the board and can be moved into, but it does not count for the present and never has to be played.

Check and mate

  • Check: a royal piece can be captured next move, from the same board, another board or another time.
  • Checkmate: in check, with no legal action.
  • Softmate (* in notation): in check, and every escape is a move into the past that creates a timeline. Not a result.
  • Stalemate: not in check, with no legal action. A draw, unless the exiled-kings rule applies.

Exiled kings

The community tournament rule:

At a stalemate, a side whose royal pieces all stand on inactive timelines loses.
  • A side is exiled when it has royal pieces but none on an active timeline.
  • If only one side is exiled, it loses the stalemate. If both or neither are, the game is drawn.

Endings

EndingResult
CheckmateThe checking side wins.
Stalemate, both or neither side exiledDraw.
Stalemate, one side exiledThe exiled side loses.
ResignationThe opponent wins.
Time forfeitThe side that ran out of time loses.

There is no repetition or fifty-move rule.
